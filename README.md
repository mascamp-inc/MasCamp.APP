## About MasCamp™

MasCamp™ is a Carnival operations management platform designed to support Mas Bands, Section Managers, vendors, and masqueraders through integrated technology.

This public repository documents the history, evolution, and significant milestones of MasCamp™ from its early development in 2024 onward.

> **Repository Purpose:** Public historical documentation, development milestones, company announcements, and platform evolution. Active software development is maintained separately.

---

## Development Timeline

### 2024 | Early Development

**November 11, 2024** — Early developments of extending WooCommerce functionality.
- Development of custom product display functionality.
- Development of custom customner ordering functionality.

### 2025 | Platform Development

- Development of customer accounts and costume ordering functionality.
- Payment management and order processing enhancements.
- Automatic split product commissions paymentrouting to vendors
- Automatically divide the funds between the platform (via a commission fee) and one or more independent vendors in real time.
- Multiple currency functionality
- Add Funds on account
- Pickup Scheduling and QR Check-in
- SMS Message Notifications
- Onsite Tap & Pin POS Terminals Functionality
- VIP and Complementary product shopping experience
- Affiliate URL's & Codes
 
### 2026 | Platform Evolution

- Costume pickup, distribution, and operational workflows.
- Centralized account and identity management initiatives.
- Operations management and ERP improvements.
- Mobile application development.
- Expanded integrations and platform architecture.
